## Method #1: Manual Research (Your Hands)

**Price:** $0  
**Effectiveness:** 10/10 for accuracy, 2/10 for scale  
**Best for:** Learning your market, finding first contacts

### Why Start Manual

Before you automate anything, you need to understand:
- Which Telegram channels your target audience actually uses
- How they communicate (formal vs casual)
- What topics they discuss
- Who the influencers and decision-makers are

As Paul Graham wrote in "Do Things That Don't Scale" (2013): "The way to get startup ideas is not to try to think of startup ideas. It's to look for problems, preferably problems you have yourself."

The same applies to finding contacts: start manually to understand the problems and language of your target audience.

### The Manual Process

**Step 1: Identify Your Keywords**

Start with 10-20 keywords that your target audience uses:
- Job titles: "VP Sales", "CTO", "Head of Product"
- Technologies: "React", "Node.js", "PostgreSQL"
- Industries: "SaaS", "Fintech", "AI/ML"
- Problems: "lead generation", "customer acquisition", "scalability"

**Step 2: Search Telegram Channels**

Use Telegram's search function:
1. Open Telegram
2. Tap search icon
3. Enter keyword
4. Filter by "Channels" or "Groups"
5. Join 10-20 relevant channels/groups

**Step 3: Observe Before Engaging**

Spend 1-2 weeks observing:
- Who posts most frequently?
- Who gets the most engagement?
- What topics generate discussion?
- Who answers questions (these are experts)?

**Step 4: Identify Key Contacts**

Look for:
- **Channel admins** — usually founders or marketing leads
- **Active commenters** — engaged decision-makers
- **Question askers** — people with problems your product solves
- **Resource sharers** — thought leaders and influencers

**Step 5: Extract Contact Data**

For each interesting person:
1. Check their Telegram bio (often has Twitter, LinkedIn, email)
2. Look at their message history for contact mentions
3. Search their username on other platforms
4. Check if they have a website or blog

**Step 6: Reach Out**

Send personalized messages:
- Reference something they said
- Explain why you're reaching out
- Keep it short (under 100 words)
- Don't pitch immediately

### Real Example: Finding AI/ML Contacts

**Week 1-2: Research**
- Joined 15 AI/ML Telegram channels

**Week 3: Outreach**

This manual research taught me more about my market than any tool could.

## Method #2: Channel Catalogs (TGStat, TelegramDB)

**Price:** Free to $50/month  
**Effectiveness:** 6/10 for scale, 5/10 for accuracy  
**Best for:** Discovering channels quickly, but limited contact data

### Popular Telegram Catalogs

**1. TGStat**
- What it does: Telegram channel analytics and directory
- Pros: Large database, engagement metrics, growth tracking
- Cons: No direct contact data, just channel statistics
- Price: Free (basic), $29/month (pro)

**Features:**
- Search channels by keyword, category, language
- View subscriber counts, growth rates, engagement
- Track channel performance over time
- Export channel lists

**Limitations:**
- Doesn't show individual contacts
- Just tells you which channels exist
- You still need to join and manually find contacts
- Data can be outdated (channels change focus)

**2. TelegramDB**
- What it does: Database of Telegram channels and groups
- Pros: Searchable, categorized, includes group chats
- Cons: No contact data, limited filtering
- Price: Free (basic), $49/month (premium)

**Features:**
- Search by keyword, category, size
- Filter by language, country, creation date
- View member counts and activity
- Export lists

**Limitations:**
- No individual contact information
- Just channel metadata
- Requires manual work to find contacts
- Many channels are inactive or spam

**3. Telemetr.io**
- What it does: Telegram analytics and advertising platform
- Pros: Detailed analytics, advertising marketplace
- Cons: Expensive, focused on ads not contacts
- Price: $99/month minimum

**Features:**
- Channel analytics and benchmarks
- Audience demographics
- Advertising placement
- Competitor tracking

**Limitations:**
- Overkill for contact finding
- Expensive for what it does
- No direct contact extraction
- Focused on advertising, not sales

### How to Use Catalogs Effectively

**Step 1: Search for Relevant Channels**

Use keywords from your manual research:
```
Industry: "SaaS", "Fintech", "AI/ML"
Technologies: "React", "Node.js", "Python"
Job titles: "CTO", "VP Engineering", "Product Manager"
```

**Step 2: Filter by Quality**

Look for channels with:
- **Active engagement** (>5% of subscribers comment)
- **Recent posts** (within last 7 days)
- **Quality discussions** (not just spam/promos)
- **Relevant audience** (check comments for target personas)

**Step 3: Join and Observe**

Catalogs just tell you which channels exist. You still need to:
- Join the channels
- Observe for 1-2 weeks
- Identify active users
- Extract contacts manually

**Step 4: Track Channel Performance**

Use catalogs to monitor:
- Which channels are growing (good for outreach)
- Which are declining (avoid)
- New channels in your niche (early mover advantage)

### When Catalogs Make Sense

**Use catalogs when:**
- You're entering a new market and don't know the channels
- You need to monitor competitor activity
- You want to track channel performance over time
- You're planning advertising campaigns

**Don't use catalogs when:**

## FAQ

**Q: Is it legal to scrape Telegram contacts?**
A: Yes, scraping publicly available Telegram data is legal. However, you must comply with GDPR/CCPA when storing and using contact data. TAPAC only extracts publicly visible information from user bios and profiles.

**Q: What's the best way to start?**
A: Start with manual research. Join 10-20 channels, observe for 1-2 weeks, identify 20-30 contacts, and reach out. This teaches you your market better than any tool.

**Q: Are Telegram catalogs worth it?**
A: Catalogs like TGStat help discover channels faster, but you still need manual work to find contacts. The time savings are marginal (20-30% faster). For most teams, manual research or TAPAC are better options.

**Q: How do I avoid spam filters on Telegram?**
A: Personalize every message, don't send bulk messages, space out outreach (5-10 messages per day), and provide value before asking for anything. Telegram's spam detection is aggressive.

**Q: What's a good response rate on Telegram?**
A: 40-60% is excellent (personalized, relevant messages). 20-40% is good (somewhat personalized). Below 20% means your messages are too generic or not relevant.

**Q: Can I use TAPAC for other platforms?**
A: Yes, TAPAC works for websites, Discord, Twitter, and LinkedIn in addition to Telegram. Same pricing, same MCP integration.

**Q: How do I find Telegram channels without catalogs?**
A: Use Telegram's search function with your keywords. Ask your network for recommendations. Check what channels your target contacts follow. Look for channel recommendations in other channels.

**Q: What's MCP and why does it matter?**
A: MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a standard for AI agents to interact with tools. TAPAC's MCP server allows AI agents to find and validate Telegram contacts automatically. Your AI agent can search for contacts, validate them, and add them to your CRM without manual work.

**Q: How do I personalize Telegram outreach?**
A: Reference something specific they posted, ask a thoughtful question about their work, or share a relevant resource. Avoid generic "I'd love to chat" messages. Keep it under 100 words.
